Engineering Healthcare Access in Bangladesh

DEWB @ Brown partners with HAEFA — Health and Education for All — to support NIROG+, the electronic medical record system powering free healthcare for refugee camps, garment-factory workers, and rural communities in Bangladesh.

Background

Who We Work With

HAEFA is a US-based 501(c)(3) non-profit, founded by Dr. Ruhul Abid, a physician-researcher at Brown's Warren Alpert Medical School. Its mission: free, quality, comprehensive healthcare for marginalized and underserved people — delivered sustainably, with technology and innovation at the center.

The Need

Non-communicable diseases — diabetes, hypertension, asthma/COPD, cervical cancer, mental health conditions — are the fastest-growing health burden among displaced and low-income populations in Bangladesh, where paper records and unreliable connectivity make long-term care nearly impossible.

The Tool — NIROG+

HAEFA's electronic medical record system gives every patient a barcode-identified digital health card. Clinics run on solar-powered tablets, work offline all day, and sync records centrally — so a patient's history follows them, and chronic care becomes follow-up, not guesswork.

Impact

Beyond the numbers: safer prescribing through drug-interaction checks, WHO-standard ICD-10 diagnoses on every tablet, and follow-up care that no longer depends on a paper card surviving the camps.

Project Status

Where DEWB Comes In

Doctors & Engineers Without Borders at Brown joined HAEFA's engineering effort in late 2025. After completing HAEFA's compliance and confidentiality requirements, our team gained access to the system in Spring 2026.
